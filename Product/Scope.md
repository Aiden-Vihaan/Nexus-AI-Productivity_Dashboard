NEXUS — Product Scope

In Scope

The initial NEXUS product will include:

- personal task management
- project management
- goals and milestones
- intelligent prioritization
- AI task decomposition
- daily planning
- contextual recommendations
- focus sessions
- productivity analytics
- behavioral insights
- AI copilot
- explainable recommendations
- calendar-aware planning
- authentication
- user preferences
- accessibility
- production deployment
- automated testing
- AI evaluation

Out of Scope for Version 1

The following will not be implemented in the initial version:

- enterprise team management
- payroll
- invoicing
- financial management
- social networking
- public task sharing
- project-management collaboration suites
- email client replacement
- full document editor
- general-purpose chatbot
- medical or psychological productivity assessment
- automated employment decisions
- invasive employee monitoring

Product Boundary

NEXUS is a personal productivity intelligence system.

It is not intended to become a replacement for every application used for communication, documentation, finance, or collaboration.

Its primary responsibility is:

«Helping an individual understand, prioritize, plan, execute, and learn from their work.»

Scope Rule

Any feature proposed during development must satisfy at least one of the following:

1. Improves understanding of work.
2. Improves prioritization.
3. Improves planning.
4. Improves execution.
5. Improves productivity intelligence.
6. Improves system reliability, accessibility, security, or usability.

Features that do not support the core product direction should be rejected or deferred.
