# Overview

NEXUS is an AI-powered productivity intelligence platform
designed to understand user context, predict productivity
patterns, recommend interventions, and continuously learn
from outcomes.

Unlike traditional productivity applications, NEXUS is built
around a closed intelligence loop.

```text
Observe
  ↓
Interpret
  ↓
Predict
  ↓
Recommend
  ↓
User Decision
  ↓
Action
  ↓
Outcome
  ↓
Learning
  ↓
Adaptation
