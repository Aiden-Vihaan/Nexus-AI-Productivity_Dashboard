NEXUS — Design Principles

01. Clarity Over Decoration

Every visual element should contribute to understanding or action.

---

02. Context Before Complexity

Show the information necessary for the current decision before exposing deeper detail.

---

03. Intelligence Should Be Quiet

AI should assist the user without constantly announcing its presence.

---

04. Motion Has Meaning

Animations must communicate change, relationships, or feedback.

---

05. Density Should Adapt

Information density should change according to user context.

The Command Center can be information-rich.

Focus Mode should be minimal.

---

06. Human Control

AI recommendations should remain understandable, reviewable, and controllable.

---

07. Progressive Disclosure

Advanced information should become available when needed rather than being displayed immediately.

---

08. Consistency Creates Intelligence

Repeated interactions should behave predictably throughout the product.

---

09. Data Must Explain Itself

Metrics should communicate meaning, not merely numbers.

---

10. Future Without Fiction

NEXUS should feel technologically advanced while remaining believable as a real product.

---

11. Accessibility Is Structural

Accessibility should be part of component architecture rather than a final-stage adjustment.

---

12. Every Pixel Has a Job

Visual complexity must earn its place.
