---

2. Layout Philosophy

NEXUS should use generous whitespace around major decisions while allowing higher density within analytical areas.

---

3. Content Width

Primary application content should generally remain within:

"1200–1440px"

depending on viewport size.

---

4. Grid

Desktop:

12-column grid.

Tablet:

8-column grid.

Mobile:

4-column grid.

---

5. Page Padding

Desktop:

32–48px

Tablet:

24–32px

Mobile:

16–20px

---

6. Radius

Primary radius:

"12px"

Small:

"8px"

Large:

"16px"

Pill:

"999px"

Avoid excessive rounding.

Not every element should look like a floating pill.

---

7. Elevation

NEXUS uses subtle elevation.

Default:

flat surface

Raised:

subtle shadow

Floating:

stronger shadow

The interface should rely more on:

- spacing,
- background differentiation,
- borders

than heavy shadows.

---

8. Layout Principle

Use proximity to establish relationships.

If two pieces of information belong together, their spatial relationship should communicate that before borders or containers are required.
