NEXUS — Command Center Design Decisions

Decision 01 — Continuous Layout Instead of Card Grid

Decision

The Command Center avoids excessive independent cards.

Reason

A conventional card grid increases visual fragmentation and makes every metric appear equally important.

NEXUS requires a stronger information hierarchy.

---

Decision 02 — Next Best Action as Visual Focal Point

Decision

The recommended next action receives the strongest visual emphasis.

Reason

The primary purpose of the Command Center is decision support rather than passive information display.

---

Decision 03 — AI Reasoning Is Progressive

Decision

AI explanations remain collapsed by default and can be expanded.

Reason

Showing all reasoning immediately increases cognitive load.

Progressive disclosure preserves simplicity while maintaining transparency.

---

Decision 04 — Capacity Is Represented Temporally

Decision

Workload is represented through time and capacity rather than only percentages.

Reason

Productivity is fundamentally constrained by available time.

---

Decision 05 — Minimal Futurism

Decision

The interface avoids decorative futuristic effects.

Reason

The product should communicate technological sophistication through interaction and intelligence rather than visual clichés.

---

Decision 06 — Human-Controlled AI

Decision

Consequential AI actions require user review or appropriate confirmation.

Reason

Users need agency and predictable control over automated planning decisions.
