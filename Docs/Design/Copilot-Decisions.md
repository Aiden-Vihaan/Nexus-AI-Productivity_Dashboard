# NEXUS — Copilot Design Decisions

## Decision 01 — Copilot Is Not a Generic Chatbot

The Copilot is specifically designed around NEXUS productivity workflows.

---

## Decision 02 — Context Is Visible

Users can inspect the major information used to generate an answer.

---

## Decision 03 — Structured Responses

Important AI responses use structured cards, recommendations, and actions rather than text alone.

---

## Decision 04 — Confirmation Before State Changes

The AI proposes modifications before executing them.

---

## Decision 05 — Explainability

Recommendations expose the major factors influencing the result.

---

## Decision 06 — Graceful Uncertainty

The AI communicates uncertainty when available information is insufficient.

---

## Decision 07 — AI Failure Must Not Break Productivity

Core task and scheduling functionality should remain usable without the AI service.

---

## Decision 08 — Progressive Disclosure

Advanced AI details remain available without overwhelming the primary workflow.
