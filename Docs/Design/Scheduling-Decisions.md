# NEXUS — Scheduling Design Decisions

## Decision 01 — Schedule Around Capacity

NEXUS does not treat all free calendar time as productive capacity.

Available capacity accounts for commitments, buffers, and sustainable workload.

---

## Decision 02 — Preserve Empty Time

Unscheduled time is intentionally preserved to accommodate uncertainty and recovery.

---

## Decision 03 — Recommendations Are Reviewable

NEXUS proposes schedule changes rather than silently applying them.

---

## Decision 04 — Explainability

Scheduling recommendations expose the major factors behind the proposed time window.

---

## Decision 05 — Capacity Over Utilization

The objective is not to maximize calendar utilization.

The objective is to create a realistic plan that can survive interruptions and uncertainty.

---

## Decision 06 — Cognitive Context

High-cognitive-load tasks should preferentially be placed in suitable focus windows.

---

## Decision 07 — Replanning Is Continuous

Schedules should be recalculated when meaningful changes occur rather than remaining static.

---

## Decision 08 — User Control

Users can lock events and override recommendations.

User decisions take precedence over system suggestions.
