NEXUS — User Personas

Version: 0.1
Status: Design Hypothesis

«These personas represent design hypotheses derived from the initial product and competitive research. They are not presented as validated research findings.»

---

Primary Persona — Alex

Profile

Name: Alex Morgan
Age: 28
Occupation: Software Engineer / Knowledge Worker
Work Style: Project-based, digitally intensive, highly autonomous

Alex regularly works on several projects simultaneously.

A typical week may contain:

- long-term projects,
- short-term deadlines,
- meetings,
- learning,
- administrative work,
- personal commitments,
- unexpected requests.

Alex already uses productivity software but frequently feels that the system creates another layer of work.

---

Goals

Alex wants to:

- know what deserves attention,
- maintain realistic plans,
- avoid deadline surprises,
- protect deep-work time,
- understand recurring productivity problems,
- make progress across multiple projects,
- reduce planning overhead.

---

Behaviors

Alex tends to:

- create tasks quickly,
- underestimate complex work,
- move unfinished tasks repeatedly,
- prioritize urgent tasks over important ones,
- switch contexts when blocked,
- adjust plans throughout the day,
- review productivity inconsistently.

---

Frustrations

1. Too Many Decisions

Alex spends significant mental energy deciding what to work on rather than actually doing the work.

2. Unrealistic Planning

Tasks frequently take longer than expected.

3. Fragmented Context

Relevant information is distributed across tasks, projects, calendars, and notes.

4. Weak Feedback

Alex can see completed tasks but does not clearly understand why some weeks are productive and others are not.

5. Generic Recommendations

Automated suggestions often feel disconnected from the user's actual situation.

---

Needs

Alex needs a system that can:

- understand work context,
- identify priorities,
- account for available capacity,
- recognize deadline risk,
- adapt plans,
- explain recommendations,
- support focused execution,
- reveal meaningful behavioral patterns.

---

Trust Requirements

Alex will not blindly trust automation.

The system must make it possible to understand:

- why something was recommended,
- which factors influenced the recommendation,
- what will happen if an action is accepted,
- and how to override the system.

---

Technology Expectations

Alex expects:

- fast interaction,
- keyboard shortcuts,
- responsive interfaces,
- reliable synchronization,
- clear feedback,
- minimal friction,
- high-quality visual design.

The product should feel like a professional tool rather than a consumer toy.

---

Secondary Archetype — The Planner

The Planner enjoys organization and deliberately structures their work.

Needs

- advanced planning,
- workload visualization,
- project organization,
- scheduling,
- progress tracking.

Risk

The Planner may be overwhelmed by excessive automation.

NEXUS response

Give the Planner control while allowing intelligent recommendations to accelerate planning.

---

Secondary Archetype — The Reactive Worker

The Reactive Worker rarely plans far ahead and responds primarily to immediate demands.

Needs

- rapid prioritization,
- deadline awareness,
- overload detection,
- simple next-action recommendations.

NEXUS response

Reduce the amount of planning required while gradually introducing useful structure.

---

Secondary Archetype — The Reflective Optimizer

The Reflective Optimizer is interested in understanding how they work.

Needs

- behavioral analytics,
- productivity trends,
- planning accuracy,
- long-term insights,
- experimentation.

NEXUS response

Provide meaningful intelligence without turning the product into a vanity metrics dashboard.

---

Persona Design Principle

NEXUS should not force every user into the same productivity methodology.

The system should adapt its assistance to the user's working style while maintaining the same core principles:

Context → Intelligence → Recommendation → User Control
