NEXUS — Jobs-to-be-Done

Core Job

«When I have multiple competing responsibilities and limited time, I want to understand what deserves my attention next so that I can make meaningful progress without constantly replanning my entire day.»

---

Functional Jobs

JTBD-01 — Determine What Matters

When I have several tasks competing for attention,

I want the system to identify which work has the greatest current importance,

so that I don't have to manually compare every task.

---

JTBD-02 — Build a Realistic Plan

When I know what needs to be done,

I want to understand what can realistically fit into my available time,

so that I don't create an impossible schedule.

---

JTBD-03 — Understand Why Something Is Recommended

When the system suggests a task,

I want to understand why it was selected,

so that I can decide whether I trust the recommendation.

---

JTBD-04 — Recover From Disruption

When my original plan becomes unrealistic,

I want the system to help me rebuild the plan,

so that one interruption does not destabilize the entire day.

---

JTBD-05 — Break Down Complex Work

When I have a large or ambiguous objective,

I want help turning it into manageable work,

so that I can begin execution without spending excessive time planning.

---

JTBD-06 — Focus

When I start an important task,

I want a distraction-resistant environment with a clear objective,

so that I can maintain attention.

---

JTBD-07 — Learn From My Behavior

When I repeatedly struggle with certain types of work,

I want the system to identify patterns,

so that I can change how I approach them.

---

Emotional Jobs

NEXUS should help users feel:

- clear,
- confident,
- in control,
- focused,
- informed,
- capable.

It should reduce feelings of:

- overwhelm,
- uncertainty,
- planning fatigue,
- deadline anxiety,
- loss of control.

---

Social Jobs

Users may also want to feel:

- reliable,
- organized,
- competent,
- consistent,
- capable of managing complex responsibilities.

---

Desired Transformation

Before NEXUS

«"I have too many things to do. What should I even start with?"»

After NEXUS

«"I understand the situation. I know what matters now, why it matters, and what I can realistically accomplish."»
