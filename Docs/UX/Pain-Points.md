NEXUS — User Pain Points

1. Priority Overload

Users may have dozens of tasks but limited attention.

The problem is not lack of information.

The problem is deciding which information matters now.

Design implication

NEXUS should surface a small number of high-value decisions instead of displaying every task with equal visual weight.

---

2. Planning Friction

Creating a plan requires users to estimate:

- effort,
- availability,
- importance,
- deadlines,
- dependencies,
- and interruptions.

Design implication

NEXUS should automate computationally difficult parts of planning while preserving user control.

---

3. Planning Drift

Plans become inaccurate when:

- tasks take longer,
- meetings change,
- unexpected work appears,
- focus is interrupted,
- priorities change.

Design implication

Planning should be dynamic rather than static.

---

4. Context Fragmentation

Important information may be distributed across:

- tasks,
- projects,
- calendars,
- notes,
- reminders,
- and memory.

Design implication

NEXUS should construct a unified context model.

---

5. Task Ambiguity

Large objectives can be difficult to begin because the user does not know the next concrete action.

Design implication

AI task decomposition should convert ambiguous objectives into actionable steps.

---

6. Invisible Productivity Patterns

Users often know whether they completed tasks but not why they succeeded or struggled.

Design implication

Analytics should focus on interpretable patterns rather than vanity metrics.

---

7. Automation Trust

Users may distrust recommendations when the system behaves like a black box.

Design implication

Recommendations should expose relevant reasoning and provide mechanisms for correction.

---

8. Dashboard Overload

Adding more metrics does not automatically create more insight.

Design implication

The interface should prioritize decisions over data density.

---

9. Loss of Agency

Aggressive automation can make users feel that the software controls their schedule.

Design implication

NEXUS should remain an intelligent collaborator rather than an invisible controller.

---

10. Planning as Another Task

A productivity system can paradoxically become another responsibility.

Design implication

NEXUS should continuously reduce the amount of manual maintenance required from users.
