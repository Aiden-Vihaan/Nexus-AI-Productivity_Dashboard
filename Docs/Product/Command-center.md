# NEXUS Command Center

## Product Concept

The NEXUS Command Center is the primary interface of the
NEXUS AI productivity system.

It is intentionally designed as an intelligent command
center rather than a traditional productivity dashboard.

---

# Interface Philosophy

Traditional productivity software asks:

> What do you want to do?

NEXUS asks:

> What is happening with you right now, and what should happen next?
