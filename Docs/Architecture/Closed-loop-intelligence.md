# NEXUS Closed-Loop Intelligence

## Evolution

NEXUS has evolved through several intelligence stages.

### Stage 1 — Observe

What happened?

### Stage 2 — Understand
What is happening?

### Stage 3 — Predict
What is likely to happen?

### Stage 4 — Decide
Should NEXUS act?

### Stage 5 — Intervene
How should NEXUS act?

Stage 6 — Evaluate
What happened after the intervention?
