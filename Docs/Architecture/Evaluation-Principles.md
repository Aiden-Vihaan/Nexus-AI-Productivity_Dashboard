# NEXUS Evaluation Principles

## 1. Outcome Over Engagement

A click is not equivalent to success.

---

## 2. Multiple Metrics

No single metric should determine intelligence quality.

---

## 3. Confidence Must Be Evaluated

A recommendation system should be evaluated not only on what it predicts but also on whether its confidence is justified.

---

## 4. Personalization Must Prove Its Value

Personalization should only be considered successful if it improves meaningful outcomes.

---

## 5. Avoid Metric Gaming

Increasing acceptance rate alone is insufficient.

---

## 6. Preserve User Agency

A recommendation being rejected is not automatically a system failure.

The user may have valid reasons unknown to NEXUS.

---

## 7. Reproducibility

Every evaluation result should identify the model and configuration that generated it.

---

## 8. Statistical Humility

Small samples should not produce strong conclusions.

---

# Principle

NEXUS should optimize for sustainable productivity outcomes, not vanity metrics.
