# NEXUS Personalization Safety

## Purpose

Personalization introduces the risk of creating inaccurate or intrusive assumptions.

NEXUS therefore treats inferred preferences as uncertain data.

---

# Sensitive Inference

NEXUS should not attempt to infer sensitive personal characteristics from productivity behavior.

The personalization system should focus on productivity-relevant patterns.
