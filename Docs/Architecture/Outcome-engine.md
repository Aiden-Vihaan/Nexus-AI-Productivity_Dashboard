# NEXUS Outcome & Feedback Engine

## Overview

The Outcome Engine closes the loop between system intervention and observable user behavior.

The Intervention Engine answers:

> How should NEXUS act?

The Outcome Engine answers:

> What happened after NEXUS acted?

---

## Architecture

```text
Decision
   ↓
Intervention
   ↓
User/System Response
   ↓
Outcome Observation
   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓
